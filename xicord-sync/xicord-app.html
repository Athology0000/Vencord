Capabilities

Everything it watches

Each signal is built from what your clients actually observed and pooled — nothing scraped, nothing guessed.

Voice companions

Who calls with whom, for how long, in which servers — ranked, over the whole history of every sweep.

Who added whom

Mutual-friend inversion turns "you can't see their friends" into a provable list of who added a target.

Alt-account signals

Two identities that share companions but never once appear together are surfaced as possible alts.

Identity history

Every username and avatar a person has worn, kept from the moment tracking starts — renames don't shed the past.

Session timelines

Join/leave/move events across servers, streamed and pooled, so you can reconstruct when someone was where.

Cross-account pool

Every machine and account contributes to one shared graph, so a person nobody's own client watched still shows up.

How it works

Sweep · pool · query

01

Sweep

Client plugins walk every loaded member list and prove mutual friendships, quietly and forever, across restarts.

02

Pool

Findings sync to one shared graph — highest-wins, per-account private layers, only meaningful (≥1 min) relationships shared.

03

Query

Ask by name, tag, or id and get a full profile assembled from the pool in one shot.
